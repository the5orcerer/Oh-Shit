# Responsible Use Policy

This repository is intended for legitimate and lawful OSINT practice.

## Allowed Use Cases

Examples of acceptable use:

- Security research and threat intelligence.
- Incident response and digital forensics support.
- Investigative journalism.
- Fraud prevention and due diligence.
- Academic or educational research.

## Prohibited Use Cases

Do **not** use this repository for:

- Harassment, stalking, or intimidation.
- Doxxing or unauthorized exposure of personal information.
- Unauthorized surveillance.
- Credential abuse, account takeover, or identity theft.
- Violating platform terms of service or local laws.

## Data Handling Expectations

- Collect only what is needed.
- Minimize retention of personal data.
- Validate data before making decisions.
- Protect sensitive records and avoid redistributing leaked data.

## Legal Notice

Contributors and users are responsible for compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and organizational policies. Repository maintainers do not endorse misuse of any listed resource.
