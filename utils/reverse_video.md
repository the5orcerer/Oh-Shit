# Reverse Video

Video-focused investigation helpers for metadata, context, and archival discovery.

| Tool | URL | Access | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube GeoFind (mattw.io) | https://mattw.io/youtube-geofind/ | Free | Finds geotagged YouTube videos by map area. |
| DownSub | http://downsub.com/ | Free | Extracts subtitles/transcripts from supported videos. |
| YouTube Thumbnail Pattern (ytimg) | http://i.ytimg.com/vi/#/0.jpg | Free | Pattern for accessing YouTube thumbnail variants. |
| Internet Archive Video Collection | https://archive.org/details/movies | Free | Archive search for historical and public-domain videos. |
